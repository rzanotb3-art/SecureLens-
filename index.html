🔑 Password Safety Tips

  • Use long, unique passwords for your accounts.
  • Avoid using personal information in your passwords.
  • Enable multi-factor authentication whenever possible.
  • Consider using a trusted password manager.
  • Never share your passwords with other people.

🎣 How to Recognize Phishing

  • Check the sender's email address carefully.
  • Be cautious of urgent messages asking for personal information.
  • Inspect links before opening them.
  • Never enter your password on suspicious websites.
  • Verify unusual requests through an official communication channel.

🔒 Protect Your Social Media Privacy

  • Review your account privacy settings regularly.
  • Avoid sharing sensitive personal information publicly.
  • Enable two-factor authentication.
  • Only accept requests from people you trust.
  • Think carefully before posting personal photos or details.
